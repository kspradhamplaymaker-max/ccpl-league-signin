Privacy

CCPL HYD Season 2 League Portal, operated by Centre Court

The League Portal is an internal tool for people working on the Centre Court Pickleball League (CCPL) Hyderabad Season 2, including the organising team and its vendors. This page explains what information the portal handles and how it is used.

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name and email address. We do not receive your password or access anything else in your Google account.
  • What you enter in your profile: phone number, role, vertical, who you report to, optional social media links, and any media, write-ups or links you add.
  • What you upload: collateral and documents you add to the portal.
  • A record of changes: who added, edited or deleted an item, and when. We do not track how often you sign in.

How it is used

  • To let you sign in and to give you the access level the league has approved.
  • To run the league team directory, so colleagues can find and contact each other.
  • To share league and sponsor collateral with the people who need it.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or send you marketing email.

Who can see it

  • Your name, role, vertical, reporting line, social links and profile media are visible to signed-in members of the league team.
  • Your phone number and email address are visible only to league management, operations and heads of department.
  • Finance and legal documents are visible only to people given Finance & Legal access.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored in Google Sheets and Google Drive in an account controlled by Centre Court. Your browser keeps a sign-in key on your device so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it.

Keeping and removing your information

Your information is kept while you work with the league. You can edit your profile at any time in the portal. To have your profile and uploads removed, contact us at the address below.
